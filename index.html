Discover the Best South India Tour Packages

South India packs temple towns, hill stations, beaches and backwaters into a compact region, so one well-planned route can cover very different landscapes.

Rameswaram and Kanyakumari

Rameswaram is one of India's most important pilgrimage centres. A Madurai to Rameswaram tour covers Ramanathaswamy Temple, the 22 Theerthams, Pamban Bridge and Dhanushkodi, and many travellers do it as a one-day trip or stretch it over two days. It combines well with Kanyakumari for a Madurai–Rameswaram–Kanyakumari route.

Munnar, Ooty and Kodaikanal

Munnar suits families and honeymooners who want tea gardens, viewpoints and waterfalls. Ooty and Kodaikanal offer cooler weather and are easy to add to a longer itinerary. Madurai to Munnar is a popular road route.

Kerala backwaters

Alleppey houseboats let you cruise the backwaters with meals on board and an overnight stay. Many guests pair Munnar with Alleppey in a single Kerala holiday.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a Rameswaram tour package?

Inclusions depend on the package you choose, such as transport, stay and temple visits. Contact us for the exact inclusions of your plan.

Can I travel from Madurai to Rameswaram for a one-day trip?

Yes, a one-day Madurai to Rameswaram trip is a common option. Timings depend on your pick-up and temple schedule.

How many days are required for a Rameswaram tour?

One day covers the main sights; two days allows a more relaxed pilgrimage including Dhanushkodi and the theerthams.

What are the best places to visit in Munnar?

Tea plantations, Eravikulam National Park, waterfalls and scenic viewpoints are the highlights.

How can I book a Kerala houseboat trip?

Can I customise a South India tour package?
